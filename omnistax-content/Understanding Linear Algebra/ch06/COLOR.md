Refines the book's `COLOR.md`; no new hue. Prepared 2026-11-02 with `config.md`.

## By root rule 7's four ways

1. Facts: none. No figure of the chapter draws a colour that means anything by its nature; the book's blue and orange time series of Activity 6.1.3 are the author's choice, not a fact, and are replaced by referents.
2. Conventions: none.
3. Referents: the two time series of Figure 6.1.9 and of Activity 6.1.3's plots, which the text names apart ("two time series s₁, in blue, and s₂, in orange"; t₁ and t₂ whose demeaned series are scalar multiples); the clusters of the k-means Sims, which the text names apart as C₁, C₂, …, C_k with their centres c₁, c₂, …, c_k (the points of a cluster and its centre wear the cluster's colour; the book's COLOR.md names "the two data clusters of 6.1"); the orthogonal basis vectors w₁ and w₂ of the plane of Figures 6.3.9 and 6.3.14, named apart in the caption and the text; the basis vectors v₁, v₂ (and v₃) of 6.4, whose derived vectors wⱼ = vⱼ − v̂ⱼ and uⱼ = wⱼ/|wⱼ| keep the subscript's colour through the three steps, so the reader follows one index from v to w to u. No other referent: u, v, w of Figure 6.1.8 are told apart by their labels (no subscript to colour); L and L⊥, W and W⊥ are one category, the complement drawn hatched or dashed (the book's COLOR.md: "W⊥ beside W is a region drawn hatched"); b, b̂ and b⊥ are one vector in three roles, b dashed, b̂ solid and b⊥ dotted; the three data points of Figure 6.5.3 are not named apart in the text and wear `point`; the datasets of the polynomial-fit Sim are a choice, not referents.
4. The coordinate grid, the axes, the angle marker θ, the right-angle marks, the vertical residual segments of 6.5.3 and the vertical lines of Figure 6.1.4's legs are ink.
