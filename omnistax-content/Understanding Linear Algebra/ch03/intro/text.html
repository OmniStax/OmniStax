In Chapter 2, we examined the two fundamental questions concerning the existence and uniqueness of solutions to linear systems independently of one another. We found that every equation of the form $\kA\kvecx = \kvecb$ has a solution when the span of the columns of $\kA$ is ${\mathbb R}^m$. We also found that the solution $\kvecx=\kveczero$ of the homogeneous equation $\kA\kvecx = \kveczero$ is unique when the columns of $\kA$ are linearly independent. In this chapter, we explore the situation in which these two conditions hold simultaneously.
