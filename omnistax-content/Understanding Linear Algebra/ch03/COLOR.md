# Chapter 3 colour

Refines the book's `COLOR.md`; no new hue. Prepared 2026-11-02 with `config.md`.

## By root rule 7's four ways

1. Facts: the colour Sims of 3.3. A colour made by the RGB, YCbCr or HSV sliders (Figures 3.3.2 + 3.3.3, 3.3.17) is drawn in its real colour through `F.fact` (`facts: ["spectrum"]`); the 8 × 8 block (3.3.7 + 3.3.8) draws its pixels in their real colours and its luminance values as one scalar on one grey scale with a legend, and the reconstruction under the threshold the same way. The photographs of 3.2.12 and 3.3 are facts in themselves. Nowhere else does colour mean anything by its nature.
2. Conventions: none.
3. Referents: the two or three subscripted vectors of one figure that the text names apart (below). An unsubscripted pair such as v and w is not a referent pair.
4. Categories: `vector` for every arrow and its bold letter (x, b, 0, the columns v₁ … vₙ and the basis vectors v₁, v₂, …, b₁ and b₂ the columns of an inverse, w₁, w₂, w₃, e₁, e₂, e₃) and for the coordinate vector {x}_B, which is a vector of weights; `matrix` for A, B, C, A⁻¹, B⁻¹, I, I_n, I_m, L, U, P, S, R, L₁ … L₃, A′, P_B, P_B⁻¹, P_C, D, E_j wherever a figure or a readout draws one as a whole, for the transformations of 3.1's Sim and for the image of anything under a matrix: the image of the unit grid and of the unit square under A and its return under A⁻¹ (3.1's Sim), the parallelogram of the columns of A (3.4.5, 3.4.6, 3.4.7, 3.4.9), the parallelepiped; `region` for Span, for a subspace S, for Col(A) and Nul(A), for the line and the plane of Example 3.5.2's drawings, the line through the origin of 3.5.3, and for a span filled in. The basis grid of v₁, v₂ in 3.2.2 (and 3.2.13, 3.4.1) is ink, as the lattice of linear combinations was in Chapter 2; the standard grid is ink. The orientation of a pair (3.4.2) and the sign of the determinant are told by decoration and the readout, not by a hue of their own: the parallelogram keeps `matrix`'s hue, drawn solid when positively oriented and hatched when negatively oriented, with the signed area in the readout (the author's blue-against-coral is not carried over, since red reads as an error). Symbols: `\kvecx`, `\kvecb`, `\kveczero`, `\kvecvi`, `\kvecvzero` … `\kvecvseven`, `\kvecbone`, `\kvecbtwo`, `\kvecwone` … `\kvecwthree`, `\kveceone` … `\kvecethree`, `\kvecxB`, `\kvecxC` wear `vector`; `\kA`, `\kB`, `\kC`, `\kAinv`, `\kBinv`, `\kI`, `\kIn`, `\kIm`, `\kL`, `\kU`, `\kP`, `\kS` (3.1 and 3.4), `\kR` (3.1 and 3.4), `\kLi`, `\kAprime`, `\kPB`, `\kPBinv`, `\kPC`, `\kD`, `\kEj` wear `matrix`; `\kSpan`, `\kCol`, `\kNul` and `\kS` in 3.5 (a subspace) wear `region`.

Untyped, in ink: the determinant det(A) and the entries a, b, c, d of ad − bc, the base b and height h of a parallelogram, the scale factor k of a row operation, the row and column indices i, j of a cofactor, the dimensions m, n, p, the weights c₁ … cₘ that are a vector's coordinates, the components x₁ … xₙ (x₀ … x₇ in 3.3), the colour values R, G, B, Y, C_b, C_r, H, S, V, the Fourier coefficients F₀ … F₇ and the wavelet coefficients H₁ … H₄, the rank, a dimension dim S, a basis 𝓑, 𝓒, 𝓔 (a set, not a vector), ℝⁿ, ℝᵐ, ℝᵖ, a pixel count, a byte count, a percentage of data saved, and the quality threshold. The matrix of Fourier coefficients F of Activity 3.3.3 is a matrix of numbers the reader never multiplies by and stays in ink.
