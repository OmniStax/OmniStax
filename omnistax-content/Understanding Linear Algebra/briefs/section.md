# Section: build one page

You own `chNN/N.M/plan.md`, `text.html`, `figures.js`, `section.json`, and files you copy into `$BOOK/media/chNN/` (copy only; the converter has already put the originals there, named in your `source.md`'s `FIGURE` and `IMAGE` lines). Read `ost show understanding-linear-algebra N.M`, `ost rows understanding-linear-algebra concepts --where section=N.M`, `ost rows understanding-linear-algebra variables --chapter N --where section=N.M`, and the chapter's `exploration.md`, `config.md` and `COLOR.md`.

1. `plan.md` first (root rule 5; plan lines per `docs/prompts/interactive-figures.md` §1; value add and tier per rule 24; controls per rule 26; derivations scored per rule 29; `## Wanted at chapter level` with every anchor `<row> → N.M-<span id>`; a variables row wanted there names its meaning after `ost meanings understanding-linear-algebra <sym>`, per rule 19). The plan lists every figure of the section (running text, activity statements, exercises) with keep, replace, fold or drop and the reason; the author's interactive and the 3Blue1Brown survivors that fall here are Sims; nothing else is built beyond the plan's own one-line suggestions unless it names it (standing decision 6: the plan decides).
2. `text.html`: the prose verbatim in `<section id>` blocks, each opened by its `<h2>` (the book's subsection titles; OmniStax's own for the unheaded introduction); the book's Definition, Proposition, Theorem, Observation, Question and assemblage boxes as `div.note` with eyebrow and title; examples as `<h3>` "Example N.M.k" with the book's title; tables as `div.book-table`; Sage cells as `<pre class="sage"><code>`; figures where the book has them; apparatus out to the tables (the Summary list to `summary_html`, the activities and exercises to `exercises`); an activity's place in the text is the span after which its inline card sits (`place: {"at": "inline", "after": "<span id>"}`); each mention that names a concept, generally or in particular, in `<span data-concept="id">` (the build colours it by the concept's type), a word in another sense left plain, `<span data-type>` only for a value no concept names; every reference to a referent in `<span data-ref>`, pronouns included, a group phrase listing all its ids (rule 7). A `[Figure 2.1.3](ref:id)` of `source.md` becomes a plain "Figure 2.1.3" (the app links it to the row carrying that `number`) or, for another page, a link `/understanding-linear-algebra/chNN/N.M/#id`; "Activity 2.1.2", "Example 2.1.4", "Definition 2.1.5" and "Section 2.3" are linked by hand to their ids on their pages. Every `<figure>` carries `id`, `class` (`sim` or `photo`), `data-figure`, `data-original`, `data-original-caption` (plain text, symbols spelled, no math) and, where its row has `widths`, `data-original-width`.
3. `figures.js`: `window.OMNISTAX_FIGURES['N.M']`, one IIFE per figure, primitives of figlib only (`omnistax-web/src/lib/fig/figlib.ts`; vectors, grids, draggable heads and 3D scenes as `docs/prompts/interactive-figures.md` §2 lists them; the Diffy Qs A.1 `figures.js` is a worked example of arrows with draggable heads and a readout, 3.5 of a phase portrait under a matrix), colours by rule 7 (`C('vector')`, `C('matrix')`, `C('region')`, `F.ref` for referents, `F.fact` only where colour is the fact), controls by rule 26 (a matrix's entries as sliders with the book's matrix as defaults, a choice for the book's named matrices), cycle only where the idea has a clock (a dynamical system, a Markov chain, an animation the book itself plays). Every Sim's readout writes the section's relation with the live numbers through the `\k` macros. Data for a scatter plot is embedded from `source/ula/src/data/`.
4. `section.json` whole once (temp file in your scratch, then copy in), with `id`, `module`, `chapter`, `title`, `lead` (one or two sentences, at most 80 words, stating no result), `objectives: []`, `summary_html` (the Summary subsection's list), `notes`, `built`, `exercises_lead`, `exercise_notes`, `ai`; its `figures` rows (`kind`, `number` or none, `folds`, `originals`, `original_caption`, `widths`, `draws`, `facts`), `referents` rows and `ref` on the variables row of a symbol whose subscript names a referent (rule 7), `coverage` rows for every concept the page introduces (exactly one `introduces` span per concept of this section), uses and reinforces, `exercises` rows (activities inline, exercises at the end; `source_id` and `source_number` the printed number; `kind`; `bloom`; `prompt` as the book asks it, parts as `<ol type="a">`; `answer` per RULES's Apparatus: `number`/`multi`/`open`, the book's answer and `solution` with `generated_by: "source"`, the AI-written ones `"ai"`; a figure the exercise reads from in `figure`), `exercise_concepts` for every exercise, `footnotes`, `derivations` where the plan built one; then `ost set` for corrections. `ost check understanding-linear-algebra --section N.M` clean.
5. Build into your scratch, serve, Node Playwright in light and dark (set `document.documentElement.dataset.theme` or the app's theme toggle; see how Diffy Qs's chapter passes did it in its `LOG.md` if unsure): no console or page errors, images load, every `figure.sim` has a canvas, transports only on moving figures, inline exercises render.
