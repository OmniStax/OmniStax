# UI glossary

The product terms of the shell, fixed once so that every catalog writes them
the same way. A term here is used identically in every key of its language;
a translator who needs a term not listed adds it here, under the language,
before using it. The English column is the term as the `en` catalog writes it.
The zh-CN column was proposed on 2026-10-22 and awaits the owner's
confirmation (the rows are the planner's; the field terms of a book are not
here, they live in the book's own glossary).

Rules for zh-CN: Chinese punctuation （，。：；？！“”） in sentences, ASCII in
keys and code; no space between a digit and a CJK character (3 章), a space
between CJK and a Latin word or acronym (OmniStax 已更新, SI 单位); plurals have
only an `other` branch; a key's `{name}` placeholders are kept as written.
