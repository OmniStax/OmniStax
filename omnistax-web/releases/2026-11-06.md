**New book**

- Understanding Linear Algebra

**New**

- Dictate into the chat box with the mic button. Set its language under Speech input language in Settings. Not in Firefox or the desktop app.
- The chat box grows with your text, up to eight lines.
- Conversations list newest first, or by book with the By book toggle, and open inside the page.
- Drop a concept, equation, figure, note or anything else that drags onto a chat to attach it.
- A section's foot shows "Mentioned in" when your notes, chats or drawings link to it.
- Pin several cards at once on the periodic table.
- The concept map remembers your selection and focus at each level.
- Sync merges edits from two devices instead of asking which to keep, and names what each change holds.
- A sync pull keeps back files this version can't read instead of failing.
- Fill horizontal space, finger drawing and the drawing palette now sync.

**Fixed**

- The desktop app crashed on launch. Download it again from the [download page](/download) and install over the old copy; it updates itself after that.
- Backup import and sync pull failed with "invalid reader record" once you had practised.
- Dragging a note's tab aside wrote "note:…" into the note.
- A link in a note or chat also opened a second page.
- On a narrow pane, the periodic table's card covered the bottom of the window.
