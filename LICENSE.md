# Licences

This repository holds two kinds of work under different terms.

## Adapted textbook content

The directories `experiment/ch*/` (and any later `<book>/ch*/`) hold adaptations of open textbooks: quoted prose, redrawn figures, re-set exercises and the concept, formula and glossary data drawn from them. Each book's `book.json` names the source, its authors, its publisher and its licence.

The first book, *College Physics 2e* by Paul Peter Urone and Roger Hinrichs (OpenStax), is licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). ShareAlike applies to adaptations, so the adapted content for that book is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well. Reuse must credit the original and Omnia; the generated attribution page (`/<book>/attribution/` in the built site) gives a citation line.

The OpenStax name and logo are trademarks and are not covered by that licence. Omnia is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenStax or Rice University.

## Software

The Omnia software (`experiment/app/`, `experiment/tools/`, the figure library and the shell) is a separate work and is not a derivative of any textbook. Its licence has not been chosen yet; until it is, all rights are reserved.
