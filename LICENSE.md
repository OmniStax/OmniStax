# Licences

This repository holds two kinds of work under different terms.

## Adapted textbook content

The directories `omnistax-content/<book>/ch*/` hold adaptations of open textbooks: quoted prose, redrawn figures, re-set exercises and the concept, formula and glossary data drawn from them. Each book's `book.json` names the source, its authors, its publisher and its licence.

The first book, *College Physics 2e* by Paul Peter Urone and Roger Hinrichs (OpenStax, © Rice University), is licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). ShareAlike applies to adaptations, so the adapted content for that book is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well. Reuse must credit the original and OmniStax; the footer of every built section carries the credit line to copy.

The OpenStax name and logo are trademarks and are not covered by that licence. OmniStax is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenStax or Rice University.

## Software

The OmniStax software (`omnistax-web/`, each book's `tools/`, the figure library and the shell) is a separate work and is not a derivative of any textbook. Its licence has not been chosen yet; until it is, all rights are reserved.
